# What to Expect Before, During, and After Donation

Title: What to Expect Before, During, and After Donation
Topic: Expectations
Source: DonorConnect Knowledge Base

Understanding what happens before, during, and after blood donation helps ensure a smooth, comfortable experience for donors.

Before Donation:
- Get a good night's sleep of at least 7 to 8 hours.
- Eat a healthy, iron-rich meal within 2 to 3 hours prior to donation. Avoid high-fat foods such as fast food or fried snacks as fatty acids in the blood can interfere with blood testing.
- Drink plenty of water and non-caffeinated fluids (at least 16 to 24 ounces) before your appointment.
- Wear comfortable clothing with sleeves that can easily be rolled above the elbow.

During Donation:
- Staff will clean a small area on your arm with an antiseptic solution before inserting a new, sterile needle.
- You may feel a quick pinch when the needle is inserted, but the donation process itself should not be painful.
- Relax, listen to music, or talk with staff while the blood is being drawn.

After Donation:
- Keep the protective bandage on your arm for a few hours and keep the arm dry.
- Avoid strenuous physical exercise, heavy lifting, or intense athletic workouts for the rest of the day.
- Continue drinking extra fluids over the next 24 to 48 hours to replace lost fluid volume.
- If you feel lightheaded or dizzy, sit or lie down immediately with your feet elevated until the feeling passes.
