# What is Blood Donation

Title: What is Blood Donation
Topic: Basics
Source: DonorConnect Knowledge Base

Blood donation is a voluntary procedure in which a healthy individual gives blood. The donated blood is used for blood transfusions or processed into specialized blood components such as red blood cells, platelets, and plasma to treat patients in medical need.

A single blood donation can potentially save up to three lives. Donated blood is critical for individuals undergoing major surgeries, cancer treatments, organ transplants, traumatic injuries, or managing chronic hematological conditions such as severe anemia, sickle cell disease, and thalassemia.

There are different types of blood donation:
1. Whole Blood Donation: The most common type, where approximately one pint of whole blood is collected.
2. Platelet Donation: Collects platelets to help cancer patients and surgical patients with blood clotting needs.
3. Plasma Donation: Collects liquid plasma used to treat burn victims, shock, and severe liver disease.
4. Double Red Cell Donation: Collects two units of red blood cells during a single session for trauma and surgical patients.
