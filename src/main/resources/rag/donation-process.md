# General Blood Donation Process

Title: General Blood Donation Process
Topic: Process
Source: DonorConnect Knowledge Base

The general blood donation process is straightforward, safe, and typically takes about 45 to 60 minutes from arrival to departure, though the actual blood extraction only takes about 8 to 10 minutes.

The donation process consists of four main steps:

1. Registration: The donor checks in, presents official identification, and provides basic personal and contact information.
2. Health History & Mini-Physical: A confidential interview is conducted to review medical history, travel history, and lifestyle factors. Trained staff check vital signs including blood pressure, pulse, temperature, and hemoglobin levels to ensure donor safety.
3. Donation: The donor rests comfortably in a chair while a sterile, single-use needle is used to draw blood into a sealed collection bag. A standard whole blood donation collects approximately 450 to 500 milliliters.
4. Refreshments & Recovery: After donating, the donor rests for 10 to 15 minutes in a refreshment area, enjoying light snacks and beverages to replenish fluid levels before resuming daily activities.
